## Design Context

### Users
A solo trader scanning StonkFun launches for tokens that are new, inside a chosen market-cap range, and actually trading.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, precise, reserved.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark stone, one typeface, almost no color. A private ledger, not a scoreboard. Anti-references: LED yellow, chip clouds, tote-board type, crypto glow.

### Design Principles
- Say what it is. No metaphors.
- Filters over chrome. Repeat nothing the inputs already show.
- One accent at most, and only for the active choice.
- Show results as soon as the first pages arrive.
