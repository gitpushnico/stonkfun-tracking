## Design Context

### Users
A solo trader sitting on StonkFun launches, hunting the gap the site does not cover: tokens that are new *and* inside a chosen market-cap band *and* actually trading. Used in a dark room, one screen, fast glances, not a leisurely browse.

### Brand Personality
Sharp, impatient, focused. Feels like a racetrack tote board, not a crypto landing page.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark stadium scoreboard: pine-black field, LED-yellow figures, condensed sports type for numbers. Anti-references: cyan-glow dashboards, glass cards, Inter, identical token-card grids.

### Design Principles
- The three filters are equal citizens. Nothing is "sort-only."
- Density over decoration. A tape of prints, not a gallery.
- Numbers are the product. Make market cap, volume, and age readable at a glance.
- Empty states tell you what the market is doing, not that "no results were found."
